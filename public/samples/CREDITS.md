# Sample credits

Sounds used by the composer at /composer when a track's "Sonido" is set to a real instrument.

## Melodic instruments (piano, e-piano, clean-guitar, drive-guitar, finger-bass, strings, choir, flute, music-box)

From the **FluidR3_GM** SoundFont by Frank Wen, as pre-rendered by
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).

License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Files are kept as distributed (one mp3 every 4 semitones), renamed to their MIDI note number.

## Acoustic drum kit (acoustic-kit)

From the **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner, via
[smpldsnds/sgossner-vcsl](https://github.com/smpldsnds/sgossner-vcsl).

License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).
Converted to mono mp3, trimmed with a fade-out and peak-normalized.
